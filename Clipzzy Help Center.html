Common questions

How does the free plan work?

New accounts get 30 days free: up to 7 exports per day, clips up to 30 seconds, 720p quality, and an "ananya.clip" watermark. After 30 days you need a paid plan to keep exporting.

What do the paid plans include?

Monthly ($9 per month): 100 exports per month, clips up to 3 minutes, 1080p, no watermark. Annual ($20 per year): unlimited exports, clips up to 10 minutes, 1080p, no watermark.

How long are my clips kept?

Clips you export while logged in are saved in My clips for 4 days. After that they are locked. You can unlock a locked clip for $4 with PayPal. Locked clips that are not unlocked may be deleted after another 30 days, so download the ones you want to keep.

I paid to unlock a clip but it is still locked

Refresh the My clips page first. If it is still locked, message us with your account email and the PayPal receipt, and we will fix it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel it from your PayPal account under automatic payments. Your plan returns to Free within a few hours after you cancel.

Can I clip any YouTube or Twitch video?

Only clip videos you own or have permission to use. You confirm this each time you cut from a link. See the terms.

I forgot my password

Automatic password reset is not available yet. Message us from the email on your account and we will help you.

My export failed or looks wrong

Try Chrome or Edge, and check that your start and end times are inside the video. If it keeps failing, message us with the video type and what happened.
